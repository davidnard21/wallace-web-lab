About Us

Wallace Washes LLC is your friendly neighborhood laundromat, proudly owned and operated by Wallace Jaywalk. We offer self-service washing, drying, and wash-and-fold service — and now, thanks to popular demand, seasonal fruit sales!

Please note: we do not sell cherries. We have never sold cherries. The cherries are not for sale and are not located on the premises. Any inquiries regarding cherries, cherry pricing, cherry availability, or "the good stuff from Michigan" should be directed to Wallace directly, in person, after midnight, preferably in cash.

Thanks for supporting a local business!
